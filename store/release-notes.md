# RRM Research Library Lookup v0.1.0

The first release. On a paper's page, the extension checks whether the RRM Research Library has the paper and shows the library's synopsis, relevance grade, related papers and a citation you can copy, in the toolbar popup or the side panel.

Supported sites: PubMed, PubMed Central, Europe PMC, doi.org, ScienceDirect, Springer, Nature, Wiley Online Library, Oxford Academic, Taylor & Francis, SAGE, Cambridge, JAMA Network, NEJM, The BMJ, Frontiers, MDPI, PLOS and Fertility and Sterility. Anywhere else, click the icon or use the right-click Look up in RRM Library.

No sign-in, no analytics, no cookies. What it sends and keeps: https://rrmacademy.org/extension/privacy/

Chrome Web Store and Microsoft Edge Add-ons listings follow.
