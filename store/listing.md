# Name

RRM Research Library Lookup

# Subtitle

The RRM Research Library's synopsis for the paper you're reading

# Description

Reading a research paper? RRM Research Library Lookup tells you whether the RRM Research Library has it and shows what the library says about it.

How it works:
Open a paper. The extension reads its DOI or PMID from the page's citation tags.
If the paper is in the library, an RRM badge lights up on the toolbar icon.
Click the icon for the library's synopsis, relevance grade, related papers and a citation you can copy.

Working down a results list? Open the side panel. It follows the paper in your current tab and adds the record's key findings. Not every library paper has a written synopsis yet. Those cards still show the grade, related papers and a link to the record. If a paper isn't in the library, the card says so and offers a Suggest it link.

Where it works: PubMed, PubMed Central, Europe PMC, doi.org and the article pages of the major journal publishers (the full list is on the product page). On any other site, click the icon to check the page, or select a title or DOI, right-click and choose Look up in RRM Library.

What it doesn't do: it doesn't ask you to sign in. It doesn't track you, with no analytics, cookies or identifiers. It never changes the page you're reading. PubMed, NLM and these publishers don't make or endorse it.

See exactly what it sends and keeps: https://rrmacademy.org/extension/privacy/

# Category

Productivity

# Single purpose

RRM Research Library Lookup has one purpose: to look up the paper on your screen in the RRM Research Library and show what the library says about it.

# Data use

The only data the extension sends is the paper's identifier or the text you select, to rrmacademy.org, for the lookup, and it collects nothing else.
